🐘 Little Log Help

A shared baby tracker for the whole family.

Sharing with another caregiver

Open the Family tab, tap Invite a caregiver and enter the email they sign in with. For Google, that's their Google email. For Apple, it's the email shown in their own Family tab after they sign in. They'll see your baby as soon as they sign in.

Appointments in your calendar

Open Appointments and tap Subscribe in my calendar, then pick Apple or Google Calendar. Every appointment anyone adds, changes or cancels then shows up in your calendar. Each caregiver does this once on their own phone.

Notifications

Everyone on a baby's caregiver list gets a notification when someone else logs something. Turn them on or off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Little Log. Feed reminders can be set in the Family tab.

Deleting your account

Family tab → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for exactly what's removed.
