🐘 Little Log Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Little Log is a baby tracker that families share. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, your email address and an account ID from Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the private relay address Apple gives us.
  • What you log for your baby: the baby's name, birth date and sex, plus feeds, pumping, diapers, tummy time, growth measurements, milestones, appointments, notes and photos you add.
  • Notifications: a device token so the app can tell you when another caregiver logs something.

We don't collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing activity, and the app has no ads or analytics trackers.

Who can see it

A baby's log is visible only to the caregivers listed for that baby, which are the people invited by email in the app's Family tab. Any caregiver on the list can view, add, edit and delete entries, and can invite or remove other caregivers.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising. We don't use it for anything other than running the app.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage and Firebase Authentication), which encrypts it in transit and at rest. Firebase processes it on our behalf under Google's Firebase privacy terms.

If you choose to add an appointment to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar, or to send calendar invites, that appointment is also handled by the calendar service you picked, under its own privacy policy.

Health information

Growth percentiles shown in the app come from World Health Organization growth standards and are for general information only. Little Log is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.

Children

Little Log is made for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. The information about a baby is entered and controlled by the baby's caregivers.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry, photo or appointment in the app at any time.
  • Delete your whole account in the app: Family tab → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in and takes you off every caregiver list. Babies only you can see are deleted along with everything logged for them. Babies shared with other caregivers stay available to them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
